# Part 25 — Roadmaps, OKRs & Themes

Backlog is **tactical**. Roadmap is **strategic**. Without both, you either dream big or drown in tickets.

---

## Roadmap (now / next / later)

| Horizon | Time | Content |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Now** | Current sprint/milestone | Must epics |
| **Next** | 1–2 milestones | Should epics |
| **Later** | Ideas | Could, themes |

Do not date **Later** precisely — false precision.

---

## OKRs linked to goals

**Objective (qualitative):** Become the default invoice tool for Ghana freelancers  

**Key Results (quantitative):**

- KR1: 50 weekly active users by Q2  
- KR2: NPS ≥ 40  
- KR3: 80% invoices paid within 7 days  

Map: KR → G-1 in charter → epics → REQ.

Review OKRs monthly; they **steer** MoSCoW, they do not replace stories.

---

## Themes vs epics

| Theme | Epic |
|-------|------|
| “Trust & payments” | EP-02 Checkout |
| Roadmap row | Backlog container |

One theme holds multiple epics across quarters.

---

## Roadmap anti-patterns

- Roadmap = Gantt of every task → unmaintainable solo  
- No link to REQ → roadmap fiction  
- Changing roadmap daily → use themes, stable 4–6 weeks
